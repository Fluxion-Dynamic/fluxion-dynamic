Solutions

Autonomous Systems for Real Operations

We deploy aerial intelligence across four verticals. Each solution is built on the same autonomy core, customized for the mission.

01
Police Patrolling

Autonomous aerial patrol for law enforcement. Pre-programmed routes, real-time video streaming, and AI-assisted incident detection.

  • + GPS waypoint patrol with geofencing
  • + Crowd density estimation and anomaly alerts
  • + Thermal imaging for night operations
  • + Encrypted video feed to command center
  • + Automatic return-to-base on low battery
02
Industrial Inspection

Automated inspection of critical infrastructure. Reduce human risk and operational downtime with precision data collection.

  • + Power line and transmission tower surveys
  • + Pipeline leak detection with thermal + visual
  • + Solar farm panel defect identification
  • + Bridge and structural crack detection
  • + Repeatable flight paths for trend analysis
03
Security & Surveillance

Perimeter monitoring and threat detection for campuses, borders, and critical facilities. 24/7 autonomous coverage.

  • + Perimeter breach detection and tracking
  • + Object classification: person, vehicle, animal
  • + Loitering and intrusion alerts
  • + Multi-drone coordinated coverage
  • + Integration with existing VMS and alarms
04
Fleet Management

Cloud-based coordination for multi-vehicle operations. One dashboard to plan, monitor, and control your entire aerial fleet.

  • + Multi-drone mission planning and deconfliction
  • + Live telemetry and health monitoring
  • + Secure data pipeline and log storage
  • + Role-based access for operators and admins
  • + API for third-party integrations
How It Works

Deployment Model

From pilot to production in four phases.

01
Discovery
We understand your operational environment, constraints, and objectives. Site survey and requirement documentation.
02
Pilot
Deploy a limited fleet for proof-of-concept. Validate autonomy, perception accuracy, and integration with your workflows.
03
Integration
Connect to your existing systems: VMS, alarm networks, GIS databases. Customize the fleet dashboard and alerting logic.
04
Scale
Expand fleet size, add coverage zones, and enable advanced autonomy features. Ongoing support and model retraining.
