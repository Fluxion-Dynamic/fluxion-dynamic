About Us

Fluxion Dynamic Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

An Indian company building systems where autonomous flight, computer vision, and intelligent algorithms converge.

Our Story

Fluxion Dynamic Technologies was incorporated on 9 February 2024 in Delhi, India. The company was founded by Keshav Sharma and Arun Sharma with a vision to build intelligent aerial systems that go beyond conventional remote-controlled drones.

We are not a drone manufacturer. We are a systems company that happens to fly things. Our platforms integrate ROS2-based autonomy, deep learning for perception, and graph-based pathfinding to solve problems in surveillance, inspection, and logistics.

The company is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, India, and recognized on Startup India. We operate from East Delhi and work with partners across India on pilot programs and research collaborations.

Mission

What We Believe

Autonomy First

We believe the future of aerial systems is not remote control. It is full autonomy: vehicles that perceive, plan, and execute missions without human intervention.

Built in India

Every prototype is designed, assembled, and tested in India. We source locally where possible and build software stacks that are entirely under our control.

Open Standards

We build on ROS2, open-source flight stacks, and standard protocols. No vendor lock-in. No black boxes. Systems you can inspect, modify, and extend.

Safety by Design

Redundant failsafes, geofencing, and deterministic behavior trees are not afterthoughts. They are core to every system we ship.
